Why is this diagram different from the one in textbooks?

The Soudabilite.com chart follows the principles of the original Schaeffler diagram and turns them into an interactive decision aid.

A calculation made for your joint

The tool places metal A, metal B, their dilution, the filler metal and the estimated final composition of the joint. You see straight away how the mixture changes the expected structure.

Digitised lines, used for calculation

The original boundaries have been digitised and some intermediate lines come from interpolation. They may look slightly different from printed copies or course material, but the chromium and nickel equivalents used to place the joint are unchanged.

Three levels of reading

The blue zone is the first target of the tool. The green zone widens the range to look at. The white corridor keeps a historical fallback when the first two zones cannot be reached.

An estimate, not a qualification

The diagram estimates a metallurgical trend from the compositions and the dilution you entered. It does not guarantee the real structure of the weld metal or the properties of the joint. The final check is the WPS, the WPQR that applies and, when needed, a real ferrite measurement.

The 7 most robust filler metals

Each filler metal is checked over the whole dilution range of the process. The ranking looks first at the worst result found, then at the share of the range inside the ideal zone. Click a row to see the detail on the diagram.

The duplex and super duplex branch is still being checked. No filler metal selection is proposed yet for these base metals.

No filler metal stays in an accepted zone over the whole process range. Narrow the range only if you have a measurement or reliable workshop feedback.

A manual filler metal is calculated and ranked with the same rules as the database. Its real rank is shown even if it is outside the top 7.

Three bands drive the ranking: the ideal zone between 5 and 15 % delta ferrite, the acceptable zone from 0 to 20 %, then the white safety corridor inside the A+M+F zone.
